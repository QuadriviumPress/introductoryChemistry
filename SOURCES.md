## What changed in this edition

The wording, examples, exercises, figures, and tables are the Saylor HTML text, converted to MyST Markdown. Worked examples and end-of-section problems are `{exercise}` blocks. Answers that were present in the student HTML open in `{solution}` dropdowns. Even-numbered answers were empty in the source (marked instructor-only) and are still absent. Learning objectives, key takeaways, “Chemistry Is Everywhere,” and “Food and Drink App” notes use MyST callouts. A few figures referenced in the HTML (for example Figure 1.1) have no image file in the Saylor repository; the citation is kept and the picture is not invented.
