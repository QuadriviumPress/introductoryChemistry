## What changed in this edition

The wording, examples, exercises, and tables are the Saylor HTML text, converted to MyST Markdown, with documented corrections in the converter. Worked examples and end-of-section problems are `{exercise}` blocks. Answers that were present in the student HTML open in `{solution}` dropdowns. Even-numbered answers were empty in the source (marked instructor-only) and are still absent. Learning objectives, key takeaways, “Chemistry Is Everywhere,” and “Food and Drink App” notes use MyST callouts. Comparison with the original PDF identified 26 numbered figure omissions and 25 unnumbered image groups absent from the HTML edition. This edition supplies openly licensed alternatives for all of them, with new descriptive captions and resolved cross-references. Figure 9.1 now includes its missing salt panel. The appendix uses an updated periodic table, and the adjacent data table uses modern element names; its atomic-mass values remain those of the source edition. The original 38-Calorie exercise is phrased as a hypothetical because the replacement FDA label shows 230 Calories.

## Figure audit and replacement credits (2026-10-30)

The first audit checked all 419 legacy image files against the Saylor HTML. The follow-up compared the [original PDF](https://2012books.lardbucket.org/pdfs/beginning-chemistry.pdf), including its raster-image inventory. It found omissions with no surviving HTML citation. All 102 numbered figures in the PDF are now represented; the 26 missing numbered figures and 25 missing unnumbered image groups have openly licensed alternatives. The missing panel of Figure 9.1 is also restored. No stock photographs were copied from the original PDF.

The alternatives illustrate the same teaching topics; they are not presented as the original photographs. In particular, Figure 3.4 uses a documented training exercise rather than claiming to show the Tokyo attack, the microscopy group distinguishes scanning and transmission electron microscopy, and Figure 1.4 now shows an identified saline solution. The author portrait in omitted front matter is outside this figure-restoration scope. See [FIGURE-AUDIT.md](FIGURE-AUDIT.md) for every location.
