# Introductory Chemistry

This is a MyST edition of *Introductory Chemistry*, a one-semester introduction organized around a simple idea: chemistry is everywhere. The chapters start with measurement and atoms, then move through reactions, stoichiometry, gases, energy, bonding, solutions, acids and bases, equilibrium, oxidation and reduction, nuclear chemistry, and a short tour of organic chemistry.

Each section opens with what you should be able to do, works the idea through examples, and ends with a short check you can try before looking at the answer. Odd-numbered exercise answers from the source edition are in the dropdown under each problem. Even-numbered answers were not included in the student HTML.

The original HTML edition is [Introductory Chemistry](https://saylordotorg.github.io/text_introductory-chemistry/index.html) from Saylor Academy, adapted from David W.
